Bengaluru House Price Analysis

Project Definition

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Bengaluru house price data to understand property prices and their relationship with different property features.

Dataset Use Case

The dataset contains 13,320 rows and 9 columns: area_type, availability, location, size, society, total_sqft, bath, balcony, and price.

The dataset can be used to study Bengaluru's housing market, compare property prices, and understand how factors such as location, property size, bedrooms, and bathrooms are related to house prices.

Visualizations

The following visualizations are there:

Price Histogram – To see the distribution of house prices.
Total Square Feet Histogram – To understand the distribution of property sizes.
Price Line Plot – To observe how house prices vary across the dataset.
Price vs Total Square Feet Scatter Plot – To compare property size with house price.
Average Price by Bedrooms Bar Chart – To compare prices for different numbers of bedrooms.
Average Price by Area Type Bar Chart – To compare prices across different area types.
Price Box Plot – To identify the spread of prices and possible outliers.
Location-wise Property Count Bar Chart – To compare the number of properties across selected locations.
